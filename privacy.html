Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

About Gmail Assistant

Gmail Assistant is a privately operated AI assistant using OpenClaw to help its owner manage authorized Gmail communications.

Information Accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the application may access email messages, sender and recipient information, message metadata, and drafts.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to read, summarize, organize, draft, and send emails as instructed by the account owner.

Data Storage and Processing

The application runs on a personally managed computer. Email information may be processed by configured AI providers when required to perform requested tasks.

Data Sharing

Email information is not sold or used for advertising. Information may be transmitted to configured service providers as necessary to perform authorized tasks.

Access and Revocation

The account owner can revoke Gmail access through Google Account security settings and remove locally stored data.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
